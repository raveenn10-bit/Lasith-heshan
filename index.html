A little clarity

Clear answers.

A few things to know before we create something together.

Ask something else ↗
01What can I contact Lasith about?

Get in touch about cinematic content, brand collaborations, social media campaigns, event announcing and media appearances. Share your brief so the scope can be discussed.

02How do I enquire about a booking?

Send your event or project date, location, the type of work and a short brief through WhatsApp on 076 595 6303. For calls, use 076 225 5671.

03Where is Lasith based?

Lasith is based in Galle, Sri Lanka. Include your event or shoot location in your enquiry to discuss travel and availability.

04How much does a project cost?

Request a quote with your brief, expected deliverables, event or shoot duration, location and timeline. Pricing and the final scope can then be agreed directly.

05What should I include in a brand collaboration brief?

Include your brand, campaign objective, audience, content formats, number of deliverables, deadline and intended usage. Reference images or videos can help communicate the creative direction.

06Does sending an enquiry confirm my booking?

No. An enquiry starts the conversation. Confirm the date, availability, scope and booking arrangements directly with Lasith before treating a booking as agreed.
